  • Valid CNIC copy
  • Recent paid electricity bill for your residence (not older than 3 months)
  • Latest salary slip
  • Employer's NTN, office address and email
  • Your active mobile number (registered against your own CNIC) and personal email
  • Valid CNIC copy
  • Recent electricity bill for the business premises
  • Blank business letterhead
  • Property ownership papers OR a rental agreement (on Rs. 200 stamp paper)
  • Details of the nature/type of business
  • Active mobile number and email address
  • NTN and IRIS login credentials (if already registered)
  • CNIC copy
  • Salary certificate / salary slips (for salaried individuals)
  • Bank statements for the full tax year, for all accounts
  • Business income and expense records / ledger (for business owners)
  • Rental agreement and rent receipts (if you have rental income)
  • Profit certificates — savings accounts, prize bonds, mutual funds, etc.
  • Property purchase/sale documents (if any transaction occurred during the year)
  • Vehicle registration/purchase details (if a new vehicle was acquired)
  • Zakat and donation receipts
  • Paid utility bills, for adjustable withholding tax
  • Previous year's tax return and wealth statement (if already a filer)
  • Proof of any foreign income or remittances (if applicable)
04 — WHY FILE A TAX RETURN

The Benefits — and the Responsibilities — of Filing

Becoming a filer isn't just a legal requirement — it makes several of your financial decisions easier too.

Benefits

  • Lower withholding tax on banking, property and vehicle transactions (ATL/filer status)
  • Avoids non-filing penalties (PKR 1,000+/day) and surcharges
  • Often required for visa applications, loan approvals and tender bids
  • Fulfils your legal obligation once income crosses the taxable threshold
  • Builds financial credibility and a documented income history

Things to Keep in Mind

  • Requires organising documents and records every year
  • Your wealth statement must be accurate and consistent year to year
  • Deadlines are strict — early preparation avoids a last-minute rush
  • Business filers need proper bookkeeping for a smooth filing
